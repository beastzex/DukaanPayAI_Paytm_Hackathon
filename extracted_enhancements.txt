WhatsApp AI Merchant COO Enhancements for Paytm Merchant Growth Agent
Purpose: Extend the existing Paytm Merchant Growth Agent with WhatsApp-first capabilities that act as an AI COO for merchants.
1. Morning Business Briefing
Objective: Daily proactive summary of business performance, risks, and opportunities.
Merchant Value: Scheduled WhatsApp message every morning with revenue, peak hours, inventory alerts, and opportunities.
WhatsApp User Flow: Scheduler, notification service, analytics engine, forecast service.
Backend Components Required: UPI transactions, inventory data, weather, events.
Data Sources: Generate daily insights and recommendations.
AI Logic: Cron jobs, Kafka events, Redis cache, WhatsApp Business API.
Technical Implementation: Good morning! Yesterday revenue ₹18,400. Cold drink demand may rise 22% today.
Sample WhatsApp Conversation: Send static briefing using historical transaction data.
